How I work

  1. 01

    Start from the problem, not the stack

    I pick tools after I understand the constraint. That is how a QR payment system went from a blank page to $250K+ in monthly volume.

  2. 02

    Design for the bad-network day

    Real users are on weak signal and old phones. Queues, retries and circuit breakers keep analytics flowing for 25M+ users when the network does not cooperate.

  3. 03

    Trust is a feature

    In payments and identity, safety is the product. Anti-spoofing, KYC and geo-tagged audit trails cut card fraud by 25%.

  4. 04

    Make quality measurable

    If it is not measured, it is a feeling. Taking SDK test coverage from 4% to 76% and cutting crashes by 60% made quality something the team could see.

  5. 05

    Leave the team faster than I found it

    Automated CI/CD halved deployment time and a Compose migration cut UI complexity by 40%, so the next feature ships sooner.
